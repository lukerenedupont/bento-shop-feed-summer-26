# Shop Feed Summer '26 — Cover Visuals Plan

9:16 story-format covers (1080×1920) for the 10 personalized feed topics.
One hero image + one ambient video loop per topic.

---

## System-wide art direction

These rules apply to every cover so the feed reads as one editorial voice:

- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Design for a **top-weighted subject**: the hero must sit entirely within the **top two-thirds** of the frame (ideally centered around the 33–40% line). The **bottom third is sacrificial** — a product carousel or grid renders over it, so it must contain only quiet supporting surface (counter, floor, shadow, texture), nothing narratively important. Top 12% stays clear for the feed title.
- **One idea per cover.** A single hero object or a single scene gesture. No collages, no grids.
- **Real-world physics.** Everything should feel photographed, not illustrated: true shadows, believable materials, film-like grain. Ban glossy "AI sheen."
- **Palette discipline:** each topic gets a 2-color base + 1 accent. Backgrounds always quiet enough for white overlay text.
- **Lens language:** 35mm–85mm photographic look, shallow-to-medium depth of field, soft directional daylight unless noted.
- **Video = ambient, not narrative.** 4–6s seamless loops, one slow motion (drift, steam, light shift, fabric move). Must survive being paused at any frame.
- **Negative prompt (all generations):** text, watermark, logo distortion, extra fingers, warped product geometry, oversaturation, HDR halo, illustration, 3D render look.

### Production mix

- **Fully generated:** topics 1, 2 (scene), 5, 8, 9, 10 — mood-driven worlds where a stylized environment is the point.
- **Real product comp / product-faithful:** topics 3, 4, 6, 7 — recognizable hero SKUs (NYCTA manual, Stagg EKG, Yuzu crib, XT-6) where geometry must be exact. Generate the environment, comp the PDP asset in, or use image-to-image with the real product photo as reference.

## 1. City-to-trail birding
**Feed title:** A field kit for city-to-trail birding

**Concept — "The city has birds too."** Golden-hour rooftop or park edge at the city's rim: binoculars raised, skyline soft behind, one bird as the electric detail. Urban romance meets gear-head.

**Palette:** olive + concrete grey, accent: warm amber sky.

**Image prompt:**
> Vertical 9:16 photograph, golden hour. A person seen from behind at chest-up, standing at the edge of an overgrown urban park, raising compact matte-green binoculars toward a hazy city skyline. A single bird in flight in the upper third, sharply silhouetted. Tall grasses in soft foreground bokeh. Warm amber light, muted olive and concrete tones, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain, quiet documentary style. Centered composition with empty sky at top and soft foreground at bottom.

**Video prompt (4–6s loop):**
> Locked-off vertical shot, same scene: grasses sway gently in wind, the bird drifts slowly across the sky, heat haze shimmers over the skyline. The person and binoculars stay nearly still. Slow, calm, seamless loop, golden-hour light.

## 2. Sculptural mirror hunt
**Feed title:** Four mirrors that change the whole wall

**Concept — "The wall is the frame."** A single amorphous wavy mirror on a sun-washed plaster wall, reflecting something *better* than the room: a slice of sky, a palm shadow. The mirror is a portal.

**Palette:** warm plaster white + sand, accent: sky blue held inside the mirror.

**Image prompt:**
> Vertical 9:16 interior photograph. A single large amorphous wavy-edged mirror mounted on a warm white plaster wall, centered. The mirror's reflection shows soft blue sky with one drifting cloud, subtly impossible and dreamlike. Hard afternoon sunlight casts a long diagonal shadow of a plant across the wall. Minimal styling, wide-plank oak floor barely visible at bottom. Architectural photography, 35mm, crisp but warm, Mediterranean light, generous empty wall above and below the mirror.

**Video prompt:**
> Locked-off vertical shot: the plant shadow slowly travels across the plaster wall as sunlight shifts, the cloud inside the mirror's reflection drifts almost imperceptibly. Nothing else moves. 6-second seamless loop, serene and surreal.

## Generation run notes

- **Images:** gpt-image-1 or Flux (photoreal strengths) at 1024×1792+, upscale to 1080×1920. Midjourney `--ar 9:16 --style raw` also strong for 1, 2, 5, 8, 9, 10.
- **Product-faithful covers (3, 4, 6, 7):** generate the environment plate, then either comp the PDP asset in manually or use image-to-image / reference-image conditioning with the real product photo at high fidelity strength.
- **Video:** generate from the approved still (image-to-video) so cover image and loop match exactly. Veo / Kling / Runway Gen-4 all support 9:16 i2v.
- **Review loop:** generate 4 variants per topic → I review (I can see images) → pick/critique → refine → final.

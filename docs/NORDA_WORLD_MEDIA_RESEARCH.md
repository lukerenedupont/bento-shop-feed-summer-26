## Recommended first World sequence

The shopper asked for good prices. Media should enrich that task, not postpone it.

1. Short cinematic opening, shared with the feed card; sourced film if licensed, otherwise a reviewed first-party still. Keep price findings and the opening shopping rail visible on arrival.
2. Exact offers by model/size/color/market, with merchant, observed price, availability evidence and timestamp.
3. **Find your Norda** model selector: select a model to change gallery, attributed specification summary, and its merchant offers together. Keep shoe silhouettes distinct from apparel, approach or recovery products; classify against product descriptions.
4. **Underfoot** side-profile/outsole diptych. Tap to swap actual product views or inspect a detail, not a fabricated 360-degree model.
5. One cinematic film break with explicit play/sound controls and a model-specific link back to offers.
6. **Out there** runner-story rail with original publisher, athlete name, original date and source link. Editorial features are not relabeled as Instagram posts.
7. Comparable shoes with reasons and price evidence, then an adjacent-kit collection using verified merchant/product associations.
8. Persistent Ask refines budget, size, terrain and watch scope in the same artifact.

Reuse the NikeSKIMS *rhythm*: film, products, detail diptych, human story, collection. Do not copy its imagery or import all its bespoke sections.

## Sourcing and playback rules

- Prefer original brand-supplied/authorized MP4 or HLS files for native cinematic loops. Ask Norda for mobile and landscape masters, posters, credits, permitted edits, and music/likeness clearance.
- Public accessibility and Shopify hosting do not grant reuse rights. Record source page, owner/credit, rights status, associated model, crop, poster, dimensions, duration, and approval for each asset.
- For YouTube/Instagram use permitted official embeds or source links unless separate reuse permission covers native copies/edits. Do not rip social videos into the app by default.
- First selection target: one hero candidate, three model-specific short films, two athlete stories, one landscape diptych, six model/detail image pairs. This is a shortlist, not a requirement to autoplay all of them.
- One active video at a time; muted ambient playback, sound only by action, stop offscreen/background, poster fallback under Reduce Motion/Low Power Mode, captions for spoken content.
- Keep media out of the already-near-budget shipping bundle until size headroom is reclaimed. Prefer a small reviewed poster set plus permissioned, bounded on-demand video caching; no eager loading of all films.
- No invented prices, price history, tracking activity, athlete endorsement, engagement counts, or product/media relationships.
